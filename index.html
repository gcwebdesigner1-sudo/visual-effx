Questions people ask before they book.

How dark can I tint my windows in Idaho?

Front side windows and the back glass have to let in at least 35% of light. Back doors can go to 20%. Each has a 3% tolerance. Windshield film has to stay above the AS-1 line, and nothing can be more than 35% reflective. If you have a doctor's note, the medical exemption allows darker.

Is ceramic tint worth it?

For most people, yes. It rejects a lot more heat than dyed film without having to go darker, it won't fade purple, and it doesn't block your phone or GPS. All our film blocks up to 99% of UV.

What's a clear bra?

Clear paint protection film over the spots that take rock chips: hood, bumper, fenders, mirrors and headlights. It's self-healing, close to invisible, and keeps the factory paint looking right when you go to sell.
